*** DISC - Strength ...

- Sees innovation as a necessity
  - Being different/unique is good/valueable
- Boundless energy. Multi-purpose weapon
- A natural go-getter with a great enthusiasm for life 

*** DISC - Weaknesses ...

- Leaps before he looks
  - Tendency to see things more positive then they are
- Overly dismissive of negative feedback
  - Being critical (in a good way) is perceived as being negative
- Doesn't always take time to hear others views

*** DISC - Possible Blindspots ...

- He is seen to perform better when he reins in his boldness, energy
  and enjoyment of being in control to a level where other people take
  priority. He is a born entrepreneur who is constantly overselling
  his next great idea and attempting to generate support for it. He
  may rely on personal forcefulness and, if necessary, intimidation to
  achieve his aims
- As he values creativity and innovation, he sometimes ignores the
  standard way of doing something because it lacks originality

*** DISC - Ideal Environment ...

- He continuously contributes to organisational improvement
  (building/developing people)
- Activities, variety and change are constantly taking place
- He can turn a vision (a big idea) into reality

  (and if not ... learn ... and move on)

  (once). Keep on trying to be(come) a better version of yourself
  (every day, month, year). Close the potential-gap (every day; for
  yourself and the company; be(come) the best version of yourself)
- Have (strong) opinions, weakly held
- Be decent. Be ambitious. Be competent
