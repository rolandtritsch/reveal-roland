** I believe in ...

- ... *people-first*. Get good people and =unleash= their
  superpowers. Make them grow and put value on CVs. Always. Every day
- ... *purpose-first*. You need to understand why we do what we do and
  why you do what you do. And you need to like it. You need to be able
  to connect our purpose to your personal mission/vision/purpose
- ... the value of *functional programming* (low cost of ownership;
  specifically Scala and Haskell)

** I believe in ...

- ... building *balanced and sustainable* engineering orgs (where
  people *trust and respect* each other; and no time and no energy and
  no money is wasted)
- ... the value of *remote-first* (done right) as the next better way to
  build and run engineering orgs (up to ~100 engineers)

What you can expect from me and what I expect from you

- Trust and respect. Radical candor. Productive friction
- Say-what-you-do and do-what-you-say. Make promises and keep them
  (and if not ... learn ... and move on)

- Have a growth-mindset. Manage your ego. Grow. Make mistakes
  (once). Keep on trying to be(come) a better version of yourself
  (every day, month, year). Close the potential-gap (every day; for
  yourself and the company; be(come) the best version of yourself)
- Have (strong) opinions, weakly held
- Be decent. Be ambitious. Be competent

** I am a Software Craftsman

- Build and own/operate functional (minimal) systems, that solve a problem (that needs solving)
- Build with functional programming languages
- Build gardens, not jungles (managable cost of ownership)

** I am a Software Craftsman

- My dad is a craftsman (toolsmith). I learned early in life ...
  - ... that you plan, execute, clean/sharpen the tools, rinse and repeat
  - ... to sign my work (it needs to be beautiful; you need to be proud of it)
  - ... that the quality of the tools and material and the level of the craftmanship determines the quality of what you build

- Building functional teams (were people trust and respect each other)
- Building (remote-first) places where great engineers can do there best work
- Help people to find/discover their superpower (everybody got one) and develop it

- Find problems that need/deserve solving (make the planet a better place)
- Inspire others to deliver on shared objectives - Jeff Weiner
- Be strong. Be kind. Be happy
